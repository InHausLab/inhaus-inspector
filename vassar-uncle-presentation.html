What the tool does

What the tool does NOT do

It does not send anything to a client automatically. It does not touch member data. It does not guess when it doesn't know. It asks for human review.

Compliance — Non-Negotiable

Before We Touch Any Data

One conversation with your E&O broker or attorney answers most of this. This is not Vassar's call to make.

Are we a business associate under HIPAA for any of our group health clients?
If yes, any AI tool that touches PHI needs a BAA and appropriate safeguards. Carrier quote PDFs are generally not PHI -- but confirm before anything else.
What data can go into the AI, and what can never go in?
Month 1: carrier quote documents only. Never: member IDs, individual employee names, claims data, EOBs, enrollment forms. We need a clear approved list before expanding.
Does our E&O coverage extend to AI-assisted work product?
Some E&O policies have exclusions for automated or AI-generated outputs. Worth a 5-minute call to confirm.
Are there any state insurance regulations we need to be aware of?
Some states have specific rules around how broker recommendations are documented and presented. Especially relevant for AI-generated comparisons.
