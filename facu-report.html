09

Before Ads Work — Prerequisite Checklist

Facu's Explicit Requirements

These items must exist before paid ads can convert. This is not a suggested list — it's a prerequisite gate.

  • Real client reviews on GBP + website
    Social proof is the primary trust signal for a new service category
  • Leadership / team page with credentials
    People need to know who is testing their home
  • 3 verified case studies live
    He wrote them — they need to be published and verified as real IHL cases
  • Social media presence established and active
    Leads check social before booking; ghost accounts kill trust
  • Privacy Policy, T&C, contact with physical address
    Required for Meta ads and for basic legitimacy signals
  • Lifestyle photography (aspirational, not literal)
    Lab shots repel; family-in-healthy-home images convert
  • Working conversion mechanism — quiz OR lead magnet
    "Contact us" is not a conversion mechanism. Must collect email before asking for booking.

✅ WHAT WORKED — BUILD ON THESE
1
Water creative (tap water health) — 43 leads at balanced gender split
Best performing ad. Health optimizer angle. Use this as the primary Meta creative to scale first.
2
Beauty/skincare creative — 24 leads, 100% female
Strong signal. Untapped. "No serum fixes a water quality problem" is the hook. Never fully followed up.
3
Price in ad copy wins — 6 of 7 top Google Ads included price
Don't hide the price. Put it in the headline. Filters unqualified clicks and increases intent of who clicks through.
4
Google Ads CPC is cheap — $4.80/click in a real test
Traffic is not the problem. At $4.80/click with a working landing page, the economics work. Don't fear Google Ads.
5
Air Quality (14% CTR) and Radon (8% CTR) have the strongest search intent
These are the categories people actually search for. Lead Google Ads with these two, not the general brand.
❌ WHAT DIDN'T WORK — DON'T REPEAT
1
Running ads to a site with no conversion mechanism
423 Google Ads clicks → 0 conversions. The site had no quiz, no lead magnet, no clear CTA. Traffic without a funnel = waste.
2
Generating leads and then not following up past Email 1
102 Meta leads captured. Only Email 1 ever sent. E2–E5 never written. The nurture sequence is where conversions happen.
3
SEO before the site had authority signals
No reviews, no team page, no case studies, no social profiles. $8K+ spent on content that couldn't rank because the site had no credibility signals.
4
Kura email campaign: wrong offer, not wrong execution
27% open rate is decent. 0 conversions means the offer or the ask didn't land. The Kura churn list (319 people) was never actually targeted — only active Kura clients got an email.
5
Literal blog photography
Atidiv used lab coats and testing equipment in blog photos. Wrong for this audience entirely. The brand is aspirational wellness, not clinical. Jess flagged this but it was never fully fixed.
🧪 NEVER TESTED — HIGH PRIORITY TO TRY

Quiz funnel — the highest-priority untested asset
Full 5-question quiz was designed and ready (water source → home age → region → symptoms → concern). Personalized results page. Email gate. This was never built or tested. Facu projected >60% completion rate and <$25 CPL.

Facu put these on HOLD pending standalone pricing. Now that pricing is decided, these groups are ready to run. Combined search volume: ~2,900/mo. Higher intent than general "home health" searches.

6

Facu explicitly listed this as untested. Video (Reels, YouTube) and display retargeting were never attempted. Given the visual nature of home wellness, video likely outperforms static.
