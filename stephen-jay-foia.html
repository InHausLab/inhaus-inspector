What to Expect Back

Without Dr. Jay's signed consent — NARA can release:

✓ Dates of service (entry and discharge)

✓ Branch of service

✓ Rank at discharge

✓ Awards and decorations

✓ Last duty assignment

✗ Full service record (requires his written consent)

To get the full record — two options:

Option A: Ask Dr. Jay directly to sign a NA Form 13042 (Authorization for Release). He can request his own complete record and share it. The Arlington Cemetery article shows the family is accessible — daughter Audrey was quoted in the official military record published April 2026.

Option B: Wait until after his passing, at which point next-of-kin (children Stephen F., Audrey, Matt, or Alan) can request the full record without restriction.

Good news on the 1973 fire: The NPRC fire on July 12, 1973 destroyed approximately 16-18 million Army and Air Force records. Navy records were stored separately and were not affected. Dr. Jay's Navy records from 1966-1969 are very likely complete and intact.
