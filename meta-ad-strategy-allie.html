Aspen Meta Ad Strategy
Revised Approach

Building on Allie's territory framework — adjusted for Meta's Performance 5 practices and the realities of a small, high-value Aspen audience.

Aspen Market · Phase 1 Meta Advantage+ Leads August 2026

Allie's territory framework is solid. Health Optimizer, Water, Radon, Verification, and Luxury are the right conceptual segments for this market. This document proposes one structural shift: rather than testing concepts sequentially (launch → find winner → then build variations), Meta's current recommendations point toward launching creative diversity from day one and letting the algorithm decide where to concentrate spend.

The Core Principle

Run the Operating System, Not the Linear Test

✓ Recommended
1Launch 5–6 ads across multiple territories + formats
2Meta allocates delivery to what resonates
3Remove clear losers (low CTR, no leads)
4Replace with 2–3 new challengers from the pipeline
5Scale spend as CPL stabilizes. Repeat.
✗ Avoid
1Launch 3 statics
2Declare a winner after 7–10 days
3Stop testing while production catches up
4Make video of winning static
5Repeat from scratch
Why it matters for Aspen

The Aspen audience is small. We don't want to fragment targeting by territory or persona. Creative is where we create the diversity — keep the account consolidated (one campaign, one ad set), and let multiple creative concepts compete simultaneously inside it. Meta's own Performance 5 framework explicitly recommends account simplification + creative diversification as a paired strategy.

Why Higgsfield Goes In at Launch

Meta explicitly advocates a mixed-format creative strategy and recommends expanding across formats and placements. Video/Reels is a legitimate creative variable to test — it doesn't need to wait for a static to prove the underlying concept first.

If the Higgsfield ad produces qualified Aspen leads, we make several more in that style. If it doesn't, it comes out. That's the test.
